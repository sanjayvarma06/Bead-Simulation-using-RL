## A. New experiments
| Item | What was done | Result |
|---|---|---|

| A3 PPO BC-weight check | λ ∈ {0.1, 10}, seeds 0–2, 2e6 steps, compared with the λ = 1 runs of the same seeds; λ selected on validation only (`experiments/bc_check.py`); the selected λ evaluated on test as an exploratory controller | validation RMSE 2.18 / 1.57 / 1.09 cm for λ = 0.1 / 1 / 10 (DAgger 0.86 cm); λ = 10 selected; test 1.26 cm vs 1.06 cm for DAgger and 1.77 cm for λ = 1: less degradation, still no improvement |
