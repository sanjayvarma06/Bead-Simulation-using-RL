## New observations reported in the paper
* RL-PP with the wider range is no longer pinned at the bound, but follows its teacher (median
  lookahead 0.44 m, 57 % of steps inside the teacher band [0.26, 0.85] m) and is less accurate than
  tuned Fixed PP (1.83 cm). Listed as a threat to validity (adaptation, not the original method).
* With the shared lap rule, hand-set Adaptive PP cuts corners by more than the 0.18 m counter radius
  and completes no test lap.
